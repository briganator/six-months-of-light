# 25-minute Relief Society / Elders Quorum lessons: guidance notes

Gathered Oct 17, 2026 for the Lesson helps redesign (`assets/pages/lessons.js`). Exact quotations are copied from the source pages. Everything else is a paraphrase, marked as such.

## What changed (the 2026 schedule, not 2018)
The 2018 two-hour schedule (50-minute second hour, RS/EQ on 2nd and 4th Sundays, 5–10 minutes counseling first) was replaced on **Sept. 6, 2026**.

- **First Presidency letter, "Changes to the Sunday Class Meeting Schedule," Mar. 30, 2026.** https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/multimedia/file/first-presidency-letter-sunday-meetings-2026.pdf
  - Quote: "Following another 5‑minute transition, they will attend a 25‑minute priesthood quorum, Relief Society, or Young Women meeting."
  - Quote: "Elders quorums and Relief Societies will continue to study messages from the most recent general conference. Youth will use a new and expanded edition of For the Strength of Youth: A Guide for Making Choices … Quorums and classes will no longer counsel together about a specific topic prior to these lessons."
  - Quote (Q&A): "Meetings begin and end with prayer. With the reduced class period, there will no longer be time for a specific counseling topic prior to the lesson. Rather, presidencies should carefully select general conference messages that can be reviewed and discussed in their respective quorums or classes."
  - Quote (youth Q&A): "Given the reduced class period, lessons must begin promptly."
- **Implementation Guide, section 5** (PD80083674, version 3/26). https://assets.churchofjesuschrist.org/0x/zs/0xzsdza9hjotm4so4g7mnkoglmgsv99hjt7yyfwm/fsoy_print_implementation_guide.pdf
  - Quote: "Quickly begin discussing general conference messages."
  - Quote: "Discussions include personal application and counseling about local challenges and opportunities."
  - Quote: "Invite all to share their insights and experiences with the principles and truths that are discussed."
- **ChurchofJesusChrist.org feature "Changes to the Sunday Class Meeting Schedule"** (published Oct. 6, 2026). https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/feature/sunday-meeting-schedule?lang=eng
  - Under "How can leaders and teachers make the best use of a 25-minute Relief Society or elders quorum meeting?":
    - Quote: "A presidency member opens with a simple welcome and invites someone to give the opening prayer."
    - Quote: "A presidency member or assigned instructor invites discussion with a question about how the conference message helps members follow Jesus Christ. How does it help them minister to others, including members of their families?"
    - Quote: "Choose a few principles from the message that build faith in Jesus Christ and seem especially relevant to members' needs. Counsel about how the principles apply to members' lives."
    - Quote: "A presidency member invites someone to give the closing prayer."
  - Quote (Keys to Success): "Sunday lessons supplement home-centered gospel study. Meaningful, relevant gospel discussions are more important than covering all the material in a lesson."
  - Quote: "Leaders and teachers ensure the discussion focuses on the Savior and helping members deepen their covenant relationship with Him."
  - Sunday School answer (same page), useful by analogy: "Classes will have time to discuss only 1–3 of the most relevant and important principles."
  - Quote: "While kindness and love should be shown at all times, the majority of social conversations can happen before and after church rather than during transition times."

## Teaching in the Savior's Way (2022)
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teaching-in-the-saviors-way-2022/07-part-2/11-invite-diligent-learning?lang=eng and …/10-teach-the-doctrine
- "Invite Diligent Learning" suggestions (quoted list items): "Resist the tendency to answer every question. Involve others in seeking answers." · "Provide time for all learners to ponder a question before asking them to share answers." · "Consider dividing learners into small discussion groups, as appropriate." · "Extend invitations that inspire learners to improve but are not overwhelming. Follow up and invite learners to share their experiences." · "Encourage learners to review a video, scripture, or message before you meet."
- On invitations, quoted: "Out of respect for the agency of others, many of our invitations will be general: 'What do you feel impressed to do?'"
- Elder David A. Bednar, as quoted in the manual: "Our intent ought not to be 'What do I tell them?' Instead, the questions to ask ourselves are 'What can I invite them to do? What inspired questions can I ask that, if they are willing to respond, will begin to invite the Holy Ghost into their lives?'"
- "Teach the Doctrine," quoted: "it is best to focus on truths of the gospel that lead to conversion and build faith in Jesus Christ."

## How the site applies it (our design choices, not Church instructions)
- Minute markers are a suggested pace (0–2 welcome and prayer, 2–5 open, 5–20 discuss one core truth, 20–24 invite, testify and follow up, 24–25 closing prayer). The Church gives the format but no minute split.
- One core truth per lesson: the talk's "big idea" from this site's data, which is our summary, not Church text.
- Youth: Sunday YW/AP lessons now use the FSY guide. The site's Youth mode keeps the same 25-minute shape but says it's for times leaders choose a conference message (combined class, a bishopric-assigned fifth Sunday, devotional, activity).
