## Top 15 for a reverent General Conference quote site
1. **Template gallery** – one tap applies a full design (background, layout, font, finish, filter, ornament) to the chosen quote.
2. **Reverent filters** – Warm, Soft light, Golden hour, Cool, Black & white, Sepia, Faded film, Vivid – applied to the background only, so text stays crisp.
3. **Text effects** – soft shadow, glow, outline, lifted (Imgflip outline / Adobe shadow, made tasteful).
4. **Ornaments (stickers)** – dove, olive branch, sun rays, star, heart, open book, temple spire, laurel, flourish divider; position, size, opacity.
5. **Custom background colors** – solid or two-color gradient ("Your colors").
6. **Drafts saved on the device** – save, reopen, delete; no account.
7. **Undo / redo** – Canva-style safety net on phones.
8. **Shuffle style** – try a new tasteful combination in one tap (no "AI" wording).
9. Existing: aspect presets (7 sizes) – kept.
10. Existing: 25 animated entrances/loops – kept and work with all new features.
11. Existing: 21 fonts + quick styles – kept, surfaced inside templates.
12. Existing: drag to place text – kept.
13. Brand mark – site name + QR stays (it is the link back); ornaments never cover it.
14. Export PNG / looping MP4 / native share sheet / carousel – kept; new features render into every export.
15. Favorites row for templates the user saves as drafts.

Deliberately not adopted: meme templates, AI generators, background remover, user photo uploads (art stays limited to the existing public-domain Christ art and site photos), watermark removal.
