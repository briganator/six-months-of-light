These clips are excerpts (~75 s for the newer beds, ~90 s for the earlier six), with fades and level changes only, loudness-normalized to about −20 LUFS. Kevin MacLeod says his music is not registered with YouTube Content ID. A platform can still file a false claim; dispute it with the source URL and this license. **Public domain:** performances and recordings by U.S. military bands are works of the U.S. federal government (17 U.S.C. §105), published as public domain at music.af.mil and marineband.marines.mil, via Wikimedia Commons. The compositions are also public domain (traditional/NEW BRITAIN, Lowell Mason, J. B. Dykes, Pachelbel, Beethoven). Platform Content ID systems occasionally misidentify public-domain recordings; a claim can be disputed by citing the source page. That residual claim risk is why these hymn recordings are noted here even though the license itself is public domain.
